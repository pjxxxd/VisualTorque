## 2. Select untouched pairs and matched natural donors

The two competence cohorts are based only on the already-completed six-permutation
behavioral evaluation. Endpoint scenes are unique and disjoint from prior causal
experiments. Donor reuse is capped and audited.

## 原单元格 11

## 3. Pair-aligned task prompts and grouped token masks

Source, target, and both unrelated donors receive exactly the same pair-specific
prompt and A/B/C option mapping. Both prompts reuse the same donor scenes. The
donors are matched on the unchanged control value, while the relevant prompt
includes the outcome-only donor's relevant-task value as its third option.

## 原单元格 16

## 4. Confirmatory analysis

The model is released before analysis. Recovery is the projection of the patched
semantic-logit change onto the matched natural source→target change. Statistical
tests resample or sign-flip counterfactual pairs, never individual patch rows.
