# VisualTorque — Qwen-27B Prompt-Conditioned Route Relocation Audit

The structured-calculation experiment produced no physics-specific route repair at the two frozen options/answer sites. This follow-up tests one bounded alternative: the prompt may relocate scene-relevant causal transport across depth or into the answer-instruction tokens rather than strengthen the original sites.

The audit reuses the 20 frozen high-margin cue-conflict families and restores the exact prompt and option order from the original phase map as `phase_original`. It compares that anchor with a length-matched generic-deliberation prompt and the structured-physics prompt. A fixed eight-layer grid is scanned at answer-instruction and option tokens, with the original final-answer site retained. The primary structured-minus-generic tests are corrected jointly with a one-sided max-statistic sign-flip procedure; a claim also requires a positive structured-minus-original effect, parity with the old phase-map forward pass, competence, leave-one-out stability, both shortcut types, and both orientations. No layer is selected after seeing the results.
