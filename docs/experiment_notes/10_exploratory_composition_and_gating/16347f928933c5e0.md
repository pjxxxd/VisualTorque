# VisualTorque — Qwen-27B Weight-Route Mapping × Side Factorial Confirmation

The previous cross-context experiment found a reliable overall weight effect,
but the paper audit showed that it was much stronger under the forward A/B/C
answer mapping and for left-side changes. Those comparisons used different
families, so answer format, physical side, and family difficulty were partially
confounded.

This notebook gives every selected physical family all four conditions:
forward and reverse answer mappings, each evaluated on the original scene and
its exact left-right mirror. The same three-source weight-change construction,
L48 upstream site, frozen L49/L57 route, equal-norm random controls, and
counterfactual-progress metric are used in every cell. Mapping and side effects
are therefore paired within family.

Candidate families come from the earlier model-blind candidate manifest, but
families used in the earlier confirmation are excluded. Selection uses only
whether the model produces a measurable natural base-to-weight-change output
contrast in at least two target contexts under all four cells. Correctness and
intervention results are not selection criteria.
