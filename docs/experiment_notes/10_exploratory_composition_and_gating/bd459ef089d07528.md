# VisualTorque — Qwen-27B Decision-Neutral Factor-to-Semantic Mediation

The response-side experiments establish a causal L49 physical-side semantic
and its L55 conversion into an answer code. They do not establish where the
L49 semantic comes from. An apparent weight or distance state may already
contain the donor scene's answer instead of an independently transportable
physical factor.

This confirmatory experiment constructs new model-blind factorial families in
which the anchor, factor donor, and target all have the same physical outcome,
while combining the donor factor with the target background flips the outcome.
The donor intervention is therefore decision neutral: copying its answer cannot
produce the required hybrid answer. The same factor delta is tested in two
backgrounds under counterbalanced A/B/C label mappings.

At the frozen Qwen-27B L48 options-token site, the notebook adds either a
weight or distance delta and records the resulting L49 physical-side semantic.
It then selectively removes the induced L49 semantic component, with a
norm-matched random removal as control. A positive result requires the factor
delta to create the new background-appropriate L49 semantic, change the final
answer, generalize across both backgrounds, and lose that answer effect when
the L49 semantic component is blocked.
