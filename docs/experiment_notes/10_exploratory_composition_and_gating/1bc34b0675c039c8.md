# VisualTorque — Qwen-27B Product-Law Boundary Calibration Audit

The preceding graded intervention rejected a stable linear product-law response,
but its fully natural image control also failed to show a reliable linear dose
response.  This analysis-only notebook determines whether that negative result
reflects unreliable natural behavior, a misspecified raw-dose metric that ignores
distance from the physical decision boundary, or a genuine failure of the
internal intervention to track natural physical changes.

No model is loaded and no new route is selected.  The notebook reuses all 432
locked records, reconstructs the exact target deficit and post-change net-torque
clearance, evaluates natural-image competence and transition behavior, compares
raw-dose and boundary-aware predictors under held-background validation, and
measures whether the internal intervention follows the direction and magnitude of
the corresponding natural change.  Results are explicitly diagnostic and do not
overwrite the preceding confirmatory decision.
