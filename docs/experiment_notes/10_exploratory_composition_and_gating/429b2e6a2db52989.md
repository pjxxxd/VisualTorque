# VisualTorque — Qwen-27B Graded Product-Law Composition

The preceding confirmatory notebook showed that decision-neutral weight and
distance interventions both change Qwen-27B's answer through a frozen sparse
route: the L49 decoder output at option tokens and the L57 decoder output at
the final prompt token.  That result localizes where the effect travels, but
does not distinguish genuine factor recombination from a context-invariant
"change the answer" signal.

This notebook freezes the model, L48 intervention site, and the two previously
confirmed route sites.  For each weight family it applies the same weight
delta in three target distances; for each distance family it applies the same
distance delta in three target weights.  The target contexts span a no-flip
and a flip regime, so a fixed answer signal cannot satisfy the test.  The
primary prediction is quantitative: the answer shift must grow with
`delta_weight * target_distance` or `target_weight * delta_distance`, must
generalize to a held-out background better than context-free and raw-context
alternatives, and this product-law slope must be specifically attenuated by
restoring the frozen L49/L57 route rather than an equal-norm random direction.

All families are selected without model outputs.  The notebook first seeks
support disjoint from the immediately preceding route manifest and records any
finite-grid fallback.  Inference is clustered by factorial family, all capture
is resumable, and no layer, token region, component, threshold, or direction is
selected from the present confirmation data.
