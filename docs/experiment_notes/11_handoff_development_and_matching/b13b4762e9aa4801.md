# VisualTorque — 2×2 Side × Baseline-Correctness Cohort Readiness

The mediator-matching experiment established symmetric L48-to-L49 physical
transport, but its downstream comparison confounded two variables: every
right-answer original was a baseline error and every left-answer mirror was
baseline correct.  This notebook asks whether Dataset v1 contains enough
counterexamples to break that confound before any new causal intervention.

It performs behavior screening only.  Non-balanced single-stack probe-test
scenes are paired with their side-swapped configurations.  A pair is strict
only when both orientations belong to `probe_test`, neither orientation was a
parent target, and its geometry was not used by the independent L49/L55
direction support.  A broader exploratory tier allows the counterpart
orientation to come from another dataset split, but never changes the primary
strict-readiness decision.

Each orientation is scored under two A/B/C mappings that exchange the labels
assigned to the two physical sides.  A scene is called stable-correct only if
both mappings predict its exact physical side, and stable-error only if both
predict the opposite side.  The desired paired patterns are:

1. left stable-correct with right stable-error;
2. left stable-error with right stable-correct.

Equal numbers from these reciprocal patterns yield all four cells of the
physical-side × baseline-correctness factorial while keeping original/mirror
geometry paired.  This notebook freezes a cohort only if at least eight pairs
per reciprocal pattern exist; sixteen per pattern is the preferred target.
No hidden-state result or future intervention is used for screening.
