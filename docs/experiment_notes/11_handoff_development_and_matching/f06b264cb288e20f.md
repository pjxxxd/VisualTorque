# VisualTorque — Qwen-27B Torque-to-Answer Bridge

The depth audit independently localized balanced weight/distance composition at decoder layer 48, while the final answer effect peaked at layer 63 but did not pass the strict donor-equivalence gate. This confirmatory notebook asks whether a torque-specific signal at layer 48 is carried by suffix attention or MLP updates into a balanced terminal answer readout.

All sites are frozen from the prior audit. A new scene-disjoint, outcome-flipping orthogonal manifest excludes every scene used by the prior 48 triplets. Weight and distance donors produce the same net-torque and outcome change. A fourth natural donor has the same outcome but a different net torque, separating torque-specific transport from generic answer-label transport. Layer-48 source patches are replayed through fixed attention/MLP receiver sites at layers 55, 59, and 63.

The notebook is resumable by triplet and is designed for one 80GB-or-larger GPU.
