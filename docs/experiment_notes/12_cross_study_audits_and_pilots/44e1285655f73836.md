# VisualTorque — Edgewise Causal Fidelity and OOD Readiness

This CPU-only notebook consolidates the completed representation, binding, composition, terminal-use, and query-conditional experiments into an auditable edgewise causal-fidelity profile. It then freezes controlled stress cohorts from Dataset v1 and checks whether the current collection of model states is sufficient for the proposed claim that mechanism fidelity predicts reliability.

The notebook deliberately does **not** average heterogeneous evidence into a single post-hoc score and does **not** fit a model-level correlation when the number of independent model states is too small. `not_supported` means that a prespecified intervention did not pass its gate; it does not mean that the mechanism is globally absent. Dataset v1 probe splits are treated as benchmark stress partitions, not as the unknown pretraining distribution of a zero-shot VLM.
