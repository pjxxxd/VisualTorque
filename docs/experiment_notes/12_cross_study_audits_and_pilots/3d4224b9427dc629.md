# VisualTorque — Paper Evidence Synthesis v1

This CPU-only notebook converts the completed VisualTorque experiments into a reviewer-auditable paper evidence package. It does not load a model, rerun interventions, search layers, or create new empirical claims.

The synthesis is organized around the candidate physical computation rather than around individual model names: visual factor representation and binding, factor-to-torque composition, side-to-net aggregation, torque-to-answer use, conditional strategy changes, and predictive validity. Confirmatory results, robustness results, frozen nulls, and nonclaims remain distinct. Missing or unexpected source decisions block the paper-ready gate instead of being silently ignored.
