# VisualTorque — Model-Adaptive Causal Fidelity Predicts Stress Errors

This is the prospective predictive-validity experiment for the VisualTorque mechanism story. The previous same-manifest replication froze one informative causal site per model before this notebook was designed: Qwen-9B layer-16 options, Gemma-12B layer-41 answer, and Qwen-27B layer-63 answer. No layer or route is selected here.

All new scenes use controlled magnitude extrapolation: at least one weight or distance is 7 or 8, outside Dataset v1 and all three earlier mechanism manifests. Four shortcut-breaking conditions are crossed with semantic/nonce prompts and low/high torque margins. Within every model × condition × prompt × margin state, route fidelity is estimated on calibration families and correctness is evaluated on geometrically disjoint test families.

The primary analysis asks whether task-matched causal fidelity predicts held-out stress errors beyond scene metadata, label-free confidence, and accuracy on the previous in-range experiment. A mismatched-condition causal score is the negative control. The unit held out during prediction is the complete model × condition × prompt × margin state, and inference resamples these states rather than individual images.

Run unchanged in fresh GPU runtimes with `MODEL_KEY='qwen9b'`, then `gemma12b`, then `qwen27b`. Only `MODEL_KEY` may change. The third completed run automatically performs the cross-model prediction audit.
