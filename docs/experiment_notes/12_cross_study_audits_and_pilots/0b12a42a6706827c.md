# VisualTorque — Physical-Handoff Fracture Map and ICML Evidence Synthesis v2

This CPU-only notebook freezes the current paper story after the cross-context,
error-specific rescue, sequential handoff, independent replication, and
route-gating studies. It never loads a model or searches for a new layer.

The notebook answers one paper-facing question: which parts of the proposed
physical computation are supported, partial, bounded by a null result, or still
pending? It produces a claim–evidence ledger, a physical-handoff fracture map,
an ICML Results outline, and an auditable completion manifest. The newest
outcome-flip confirmation is optional: if it has not finished, the corresponding
claim remains explicitly pending rather than being inferred from older data.
