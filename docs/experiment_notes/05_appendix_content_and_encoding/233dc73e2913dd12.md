# VisualTorque — Qwen-27B Response-Code × Option-Position Factorial

The preceding symmetric-axis experiment showed that the L55 response-code
route has a robust directed cycle: B→A, A→C, and C→B are favored over
their reverses even when both directions share one sign-symmetric pair axis.
That experiment still tied answer-token identity to the order of the option
lines. This frozen follow-up crosses all six semantic-to-label assignments
with all six independent option-line orders. Every physical family therefore
contributes the complete 6 × 6 label-transition by position-transition grid.

The primary estimand is symmetric-axis attenuation. A cycle that follows the
label transition under every option order supports response-token coding; a
cycle that follows line positions supports prompt-position routing; systematic
effects of both factors support joint interface gating. The experiment uses a
deterministic 16-family subset, balanced across rescue/induction and
weight/distance conflict strata. It reuses physical scenes prospectively for a
new prompt factorial and runs Qwen-27B only.
