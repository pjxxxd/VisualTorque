# VisualTorque — L55 Code-Transition Asymmetry Audit

This CPU-only post-hoc audit explains why the preceding L55 subspace test
passed after pooling but failed its forward/reverse invariance gate. It reads
the completed predecessor shards without loading a model. The audit freezes
the two result files, reconstructs literal answer flips, compares cohorts
across runs, and decomposes the L55 effect into all six ordered A/B/C label
transitions. Its purpose is diagnostic: determine whether a robust ordered-
label crossover remains on the intersection of stable cohorts, and decide
whether an A100 experiment with antisymmetric pair axes is warranted.

No tensor-geometry claim is made here because the original direction tensors
were not stored in the shards.
