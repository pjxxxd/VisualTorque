## Interpretation discipline

Use these reports to choose among falsifiable explanations rather than to assign a story after the fact:

1. Weak distance-question accuracy supports a visual distance-recovery bottleneck.
2. Strong primitive accuracy but weak outcome accuracy supports a composition bottleneck.
3. Strong torque decoding with weak behavior supports availability without reliable use.
4. Similar torque decoding but different option robustness supports a readout/token-prior difference.
5. Only a selective, dose-dependent intervention can support a causal torque-like state.
